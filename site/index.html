Be incredibly Focused

Bubble is a pet that lives on your desk and thrives as you focus.

The problem

For too long, being able to focus meant showcasing exceptional willpower. We believe focus should come to you naturally, as a product of something you care about, rather than beating yourself with a stick for it.

The solution

Meet Bubble

A physical duck that pairs with an app. When your devices pull you away, the duck feels it. When you stay on task, it glows.

01

Set it on your desk

Pair the duck once. Nothing else about how you work has to change.

02

Choose what distracts you

Block apps, set screen-time limits, and decide who can still reach you.

03

Watch it thrive

The duck’s glow and expressions mirror your habits. It motivates through care, not pressure.
